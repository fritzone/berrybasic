MOVE and DRAW

Move the graphics cursor, or draw a line to a point.

Syntax

MOVE x, y
DRAW x, y

Description

MOVE repositions the graphics cursor without drawing; DRAW draws a line from the cursor to (x,y) in the current GCOL colour and leaves the cursor there. They are shorthands for PLOT 4 and 5.

Examples

10 GCOL 1 20 MOVE 100, 100 30 DRAW 500, 100 40 DRAW 300, 400 50 DRAW 100, 100
