VDU

Sends raw control codes and characters to the screen driver.

Syntax

VDU n [, n ]...

Description

Each number is sent as a byte to the VDU (screen) driver. Codes below 32 are commands: 12 clears the screen, 17 sets the text colour, 31 moves the cursor. Bytes 32 and above are printed as characters.

Examples

10 VDU 17, 1 : REM red text 20 PRINT "warning" 30 VDU 12 : REM clear the screen
