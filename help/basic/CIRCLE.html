CIRCLE

Draws a circle outline (or a filled disc).

Syntax

CIRCLE x, y, radius
CIRCLE FILL x, y, radius

Description

Draws a circle centred at (x,y) with the given radius in the current GCOL colour. Add FILL to draw a solid disc.

Examples

10 GCOL 2 20 CIRCLE 640, 512, 200 30 GCOL 1 : CIRCLE FILL 300, 300, 80
